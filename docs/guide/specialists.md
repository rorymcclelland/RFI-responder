# Answering assigned questions

For a colleague whose expertise a response owner needs: someone has assigned you a question to answer or check.

## Your inbox

**Assignments** lists the questions sent to you (**My Inbox**) and the ones you have sent to others (**Sent**); admins also see **All**. Filter by stage: **Pending**, **In Review**, **Answered**, **Approved**, and **Closed** for assignments closed when their response was exported. The counts at the top show how many of yours are pending and in review.

Choose an assignment to see the question, the proposed answer when there is one, and any notes.

## Working through an assignment

An assignment moves through four stages, shown by the dots on the right: pending, in review, answered, approved. On your own assignments, the button that moves it on is named after the next stage (for example "Mark as In Review"). If you are also the response's owner or an admin, marking yours approved approves the other assignments on the same question too; otherwise only yours changes. If a change cannot be saved (for example, the response has been exported and its assignments closed), the inbox says so and shows what was saved.

Use **Add Notes** (or **Edit Notes**) to tell the owner what you found, what you changed, or what still needs checking. The owner sees your notes in a banner at the top of their review list.

## Deciding the question itself

The inbox tracks the assignment; the answer is decided in the response's workspace. Open **Opportunities**, choose the response (its name is shown on the assignment), and find the question. As someone assigned to it you can **Accept** the draft, **Edit First** and **Save & Accept** your version, **Write an answer** when there is no draft, or **Skip** it, exactly as the owner can ([Running a response](running-a-response.md#deciding-one-question)). [The question card](question-card.md) explains what you will see.

You cannot approve questions in bulk, assign others, or record the export: those are the owner's.

## Getting suggested for the right questions

Owners are shown suggestions from who wrote or approved similar answers before, and from the expertise tags on profiles. Keep yours current under **Team**: choose **Edit** on your card and add tags under **Expertise & Specialisms**.
