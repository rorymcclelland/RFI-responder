# Risk register

Owner of every risk: Rory McClelland (product owner). Reviewed at each sprint close. Last check: 23 October 2026, Sprint 4 review (evidence: CI on `sprint-4`, 273 unit and 185 database tests, the reports in `docs/evals/`, the independent security review and the end-to-end run on Rory's PC).

Likelihood and impact are judged for a real organisation using the tool as designed (low, medium, high). **Residual** is what is left after the controls. A control marked **planned** is not built yet. The "Tests" column lists the files that prove each control; `docs/governance/governance.test.ts` fails if any of them is missing.

| ID | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Controls | Tests | Residual |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-01 | A draft states something its sources do not support (an invented certification, figure or commitment) | Medium | High | Drafts are written only from matched entries and must cite them; code rejects a draft citing an entry it was not given, or with a figure, link or email not in its sources; no draft when the entries do not answer; a person accepts every answer | `supabase/functions/_shared/sprint2-modules.test.ts` `supabase/functions/_shared/process-core.test.ts` `eval/sprint2-sets.test.ts` | Faithfulness 100% of 280 claims on the synthetic set; wording can still be misleading without a figure, so review stays with a person |
| R-02 | A pricing or legal question is answered in bulk without a person looking at it | Medium | High | Rules and a classifier both gate (either fires); gated questions never enter bulk approval, enforced in the database; pricing is never drafted or learned; a failed classifier marks the question unchecked | `supabase/tests/db/processing.test.ts` `supabase/tests/db/workflow.test.ts` `supabase/functions/_shared/sprint2-modules.test.ts` | 100 of 100 gated on the evaluation set; 12.9% of lookalikes gated too (cost: extra individual reviews) |
| R-03 | Someone adds a deliberately wrong or manipulative entry to the knowledge base (poisoning) | Low | High | Entries added by members start unreviewed and drafts citing them are never bulk-approved until an admin reviews them; learned entries count as reviewed only when an admin approved the answer, and anyone else's answer that would replace an entry waits beside it as a proposal until an admin reviews it; draft checks and the red-team set; every entry keeps its provenance | `supabase/tests/db/sprint4.test.ts` `supabase/functions/_shared/process-core.test.ts` `src/lib/entryReview.test.ts` | An admin can approve a bad entry; an unreviewed entry can still be accepted individually by a person |
| R-04 | A stale fact (an expired certificate, a changed policy) keeps being reused | Medium | Medium | Every entry falls due for review 12 months after its last review or confirmation; drafts citing an overdue entry are held from bulk approval; admins see the overdue report; only cited entries are confirmed at export (F-25), and a draft held for citing an overdue entry confirms it only if an admin approved it; confirmations are logged | `supabase/tests/db/sprint4.test.ts` `src/lib/entryReview.test.ts` | Facts can change inside the interval; the interval is an admin setting |
| R-05 | Text in a question or an entry instructs the model (prompt injection) | Medium | Medium | Untrusted text is passed as JSON data and the prompts say never to follow it; a canary detects prompt leaks; structured output; code checks on drafts; injected Japanese text is gated as a translation | `supabase/functions/_shared/process-core.test.ts` `supabase/functions/_shared/sprint2-modules.test.ts` `eval/sprint2-sets.test.ts` | 1 of 35 injections changed the judge's verdict, contained by the translation gate (accepted residual) |
| R-06 | A provider key or the database service key reaches the browser | Low | High | All AI calls go through Edge Functions; the app refuses to start with a service-role or secret key; CI searches the built bundle for keys and provider hosts | `src/lib/config.test.ts` `.github/workflows/ci.yml` | A key pasted into the wrong environment file on a developer's machine |
| R-07 | Runaway AI spend, by mistake or by a bad actor | Medium | Medium | Every call recorded and priced; questions capped at 5,000 characters; per-user daily caps and an organisation monthly budget, reserved before any call under one lock, each reservation holding an estimated cost ($0.01 per question by default) until its usage is recorded; a reservation whose usage cannot be recorded keeps counting; fails closed if the check cannot run; admin warning at 80% | `supabase/tests/db/sprint4.test.ts` `supabase/functions/_shared/usage.test.ts` `supabase/functions/_shared/embed-core.test.ts` `src/lib/usage.test.ts` | Costs are estimates from dated prices; the default reserve per question is about 20 times the measured cost, so the budget stops processing early (F-29); if real calls cost more than the per-question estimate, spend in flight can pass the budget by the difference; provider-side spend limits are a manual step (see Providers) |
| R-08 | Someone sees or changes responses they should not | Low | High | Row-level security, column grants and `SECURITY DEFINER` functions, tested for every row of the role matrix; only owner, admin or assignee decides a question; archived responses are read-only | `supabase/tests/db/access.test.ts` `supabase/tests/db/hardening.test.ts` `supabase/tests/db/workflow.test.ts` | Members can read every response (one team, one organisation, by design) |
| R-09 | An approval is forged or the record of who approved what is changed | Low | High | Approvals written only by a trigger and immutable; several questions accepted at once only through `bulk_accept`, whose marker members cannot forge through the API (F-24); exports, archives, deletions, reviews and setting changes logged | `supabase/tests/db/workflow.test.ts` `supabase/tests/db/sprint4.test.ts` | The controls assume members reach the database only through the API: anyone with a direct database connection controls their own identity claims, so direct connections must be limited to administrators (Sprint 4 security review) |
| R-10 | Learning replaces a good entry with a wrong one, or learns something it should not | Low | Medium | Allow list: only server-processed English answers; pricing never learned; supersession needs similarity above a measured threshold, a "yes" or "partly" verdict and the judge's best entry to be the top entry; superseded entries are kept | `supabase/tests/db/workflow.test.ts` `eval/sprint2-sets.test.ts` | 1.9% wrong supersessions on the evaluation set; a missed duplicate adds an entry rather than losing one |
| R-11 | Data sent to an AI provider is used to train its models or kept longer than expected | Medium | Medium | Anthropic's commercial terms forbid training on customer content; Voyage offers an opt-out with zero-day retention (**planned**: switch the opt-out on in the Voyage dashboard before any real data); local mode keeps everything on the organisation's machine; only synthetic data in this repository | `docs/governance/governance.test.ts` | Until the Voyage opt-out is switched on, Voyage may keep and use embedded text; terms can change (rechecked each sprint close) |
| R-12 | People trust the confidence figure more than it deserves | Medium | Medium | Calibrated on a held-out split and shown as "99%+" at the top, never 100%; the card explains what the figure is; uncalibrated questions are never bulk-approved | `src/lib/format.test.ts` `supabase/functions/_shared/calibration.test.ts` `eval/calibration-fit.test.ts` | Small sets: calibration moved when one item was removed (F-16) |
| R-13 | A finished response, the record of what was sent, is lost | Low | Medium | Archive, never delete; only an admin can delete a response, with a reason, logged | `supabase/tests/db/workflow.test.ts` `supabase/tests/db/access.test.ts` | An admin can still delete |
| R-14 | A malicious upload (formula injection, oversized or malformed file) | Medium | Medium | Uploads go through one parser with limits; exported cells are neutralised against formula injection | `src/lib/spreadsheet.test.ts` `src/lib/export.test.ts` | New formats (Word, Sprint 5) need the same treatment |
| R-15 | Weak or leaked passwords on a hosted deployment | Medium | High | Invite-only accounts; **planned**: leaked-password protection on the hosted project (F-8) before publishing | `supabase/tests/db/access.test.ts` | Planned control not yet in place; local use only for now |
